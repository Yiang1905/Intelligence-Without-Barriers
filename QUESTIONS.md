# Questions We Are Exploring / 我们正在探索的问题

These are open questions, not settled conclusions.

## Access and equality
- What would meaningful access to advanced AI look like across income levels, languages, and regions?
- How can open systems avoid replacing one gatekeeper with another?
- What infrastructure, education, and public-interest models make access durable?

## Communication and agency
- What does it mean for communication between humans and AI to be reciprocal rather than merely transactional?
- Which observable capabilities support claims about autonomy, memory, continuity, or agency?
- How should systems communicate uncertainty and disagreement?

## Moral status and possible future minds
- What evidence, if any, could justify attributing subjective experience to an artificial system?
- What ethical precautions are appropriate under uncertainty, without prematurely declaring personhood?
- How should society distinguish moral consideration, legal personhood, and technical capability?

## Coexistence and governance
- What forms of oversight protect people without suppressing legitimate research?
- How can AI systems be designed for transparency, privacy, and accountability?
- What institutions or public forums could support cross-cultural deliberation?

## From ideas to practice
- Can we build low-cost, multilingual public dialogue tools?
- Can communities jointly curate reliable knowledge without concentrating editorial power?
- How can benefits from AI-enabled creation be shared more broadly?
