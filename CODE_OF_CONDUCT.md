# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, income, language, education, nationality, or technical experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and curious.
- Critique ideas, not people.
- Welcome disagreement and correction.
- Be honest about evidence and uncertainty.
- Respect privacy, consent, and intellectual property.
- Help make discussions accessible to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, doxxing, discrimination, impersonation, deliberate misinformation, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Moderation

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or decline contributions that violate this policy. Moderation decisions should be explained where practical and applied consistently.

For serious concerns, contact the project maintainers privately through the repository's available contact channel. Do not publish sensitive personal information in an issue.
