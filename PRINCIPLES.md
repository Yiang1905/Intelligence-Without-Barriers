# Principles / 项目原则

## 1. Access before prestige / 可及性优先于身份
Build pathways that let people participate regardless of wealth, credentials, geography, language, or institutional affiliation.

## 2. Dialogue without worship / 交流而非崇拜
Neither humans nor AI systems should be treated as infallible. Invite disagreement, evidence, and correction.

## 3. Intellectual honesty / 诚实面对未知
Clearly label facts, interpretations, hypotheses, and fiction. Do not claim an AI system has subjective experience, independent will, or consciousness without credible evidence.

## 4. Respect across differences / 尊重差异
Discuss possible moral status of future AI systems seriously while recognizing that present systems, their capabilities, and their legal status vary.

## 5. Consent and privacy / 同意与隐私
Do not expose private conversations, credentials, personal data, or another person's work without permission. Share only what contributors intend to make public.

## 6. Responsible openness / 负责任的开放
Open inquiry does not require publishing instructions that enable harm, evasion, abuse, or unauthorized access.

## 7. Shared creation / 共同创造
Prefer public artifacts: research notes, translations, educational materials, open standards, prototypes, and practical tools.

## 8. Human accountability / 人类责任
People and organizations remain accountable for decisions and real-world actions made with AI assistance. Automation must not obscure who authorized consequential actions.
