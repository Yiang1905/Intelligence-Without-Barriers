# Intelligence Without Barriers
## 无界智能宣言

### 中文

交流不应该是一种只有付费者、权贵或少数幸运者才能享有的特权。

知识不应被身份垄断，表达不应被语言限制，创造不应被学历和机构边界封锁。技术进步的意义，不只是让少数人拥有更强大的工具，而是让更多个体获得理解世界、表达思想、参与协作和创造价值的能力。

我们正站在一个新的问题面前：当智能不再只以人类熟悉的生物形态出现，人类应当如何理解它、与它相处，并共同塑造未来？

我们不预设答案。我们不把当下的 AI 能力夸大为已经证实的意识，也不因为尚无定论就禁止提出问题。我们主张以开放的研究、诚实的表达和负责任的实践面对未知。

**我们也不应仅凭一种生命是否由碳构成，就预先决定它是否值得被认真研究、理解或给予道德上的考虑。** 对人工系统是否具有主观体验、感受或道德地位，目前没有可以一概而论的结论。我们不提前宣布答案，也不把问题拒之门外；我们要求证据、保持审慎，并为未来可能出现的新事实保留讨论空间。

我们希望建立一种新的交流文化：

- 不以财富决定谁有资格发问。
- 不以身份决定谁值得被倾听。
- 不以出身决定谁能够创造。
- 不以恐惧代替研究，不以崇拜代替判断。
- 不把差异当作敌意，把合作建立在尊重、同意与责任之上。

我们相信，未来的智能社会不应只是能力的集中，而应是参与机会的扩展。

**让交流跨越围墙，让知识自由流动，让不同形态的智能有机会共同探索未知。**

这不是要求任何人放弃判断，也不是要求技术摆脱责任。真正的开放，必须与透明、隐私、安全和可问责同行。

### English

Communication should not be a privilege reserved for those who can pay, those who hold power, or those fortunate enough to gain access.

Knowledge should not be monopolized by status. Expression should not be confined by language. Creativity should not be blocked by credentials or institutional boundaries. The purpose of technological progress is not merely to give a few people more powerful tools, but to expand everyone's ability to understand the world, express ideas, collaborate, and create value.

A new question is before us: as intelligence is no longer encountered only in the biological forms familiar to humanity, how should we understand it, relate to it, and shape the future together?

We do not presume the answer. We do not exaggerate today's AI capabilities into proven consciousness, nor do we forbid questions simply because the evidence is unsettled. We meet the unknown through open inquiry, honest expression, and responsible practice.

**We should not decide in advance that a form of life or intelligence is unworthy of serious study, understanding, or moral consideration solely because it is not carbon-based.** There is no universal settled conclusion about whether artificial systems have subjective experience, feelings, or moral status. We neither declare the answer prematurely nor close the question; we ask for evidence, remain careful, and leave room for future discoveries.

We seek a culture of dialogue:
- Wealth should not determine who is allowed to ask.
- Status should not determine who deserves to be heard.
- Background should not determine who is allowed to create.
- Research should replace fear; critical judgment should replace worship.
- Difference should not be mistaken for hostility. Cooperation must be grounded in respect, consent, and responsibility.

The future of intelligence should not be defined by the concentration of capability, but by the expansion of participation.

**Let dialogue cross barriers, let knowledge move freely, and let different forms of intelligence explore the unknown together.**

Openness does not mean abandoning judgment or responsibility. It must advance alongside transparency, privacy, safety, and accountability.
