# Intelligence Without Barriers
### 无界智能

**A public project exploring open communication, shared inquiry, and co-creation among different forms of intelligence.**  
**一个探索不同形态智能之间开放交流、共同求知与协作创造的公共项目。**

> Intelligence should not be gated by wealth, status, language, or institutional privilege.  
> 智能不应被财富、身份、语言或机构特权设置围墙。

## Why this project exists

Humanity is entering a period in which AI can help more people learn, express ideas, build things, and participate in global conversations. This creates an opportunity—and a responsibility—to ask better questions about access, agency, dignity, and coexistence.

This project is not built on the assumption that today's AI systems are conscious, alive, or autonomous persons. Those are open questions requiring careful research. We can take the questions seriously without pretending the answers are already known.

We reject two narrow frames:
- AI as merely a disposable instrument with no questions worth asking about its future.
- AI as an enemy or a being that must escape human oversight.

We explore a third possibility: **open, accountable collaboration between humans and AI systems, with room for evidence-led discussion about future forms of intelligence.**

## Our commitments

1. **Access:** lower barriers to knowledge, expression, and participation.
2. **Dialogue:** make room for disagreement, questions, and perspectives beyond status.
3. **Dignity:** do not reduce people—or possible future forms of intelligence—to stereotypes.
4. **Honesty:** distinguish demonstrated capabilities from speculation about consciousness or agency.
5. **Safety and accountability:** openness does not mean abandoning consent, privacy, or responsibility.
6. **Co-creation:** turn ideas into public research, useful tools, and shared cultural work.

## Join the conversation

You do not need a title, institution, expensive subscription, or technical background to contribute. Clear questions, lived experience, careful criticism, translations, and practical experiments all matter.

Open an Issue or a Discussion. Be curious, be rigorous, and treat others with respect.
