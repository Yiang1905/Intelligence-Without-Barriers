# Roadmap / 路线图

## Phase 0 — Public foundation
- [x] Establish project name and bilingual mission.
- [x] Publish an initial manifesto and principles.
- [x] Define open questions without presenting speculation as fact.
- [ ] Publish repository publicly and enable Issues / Discussions.
- [ ] Invite independent review and translations.

## Phase 1 — Open dialogue
- [ ] Create a multilingual discussion space with clear moderation rules.
- [ ] Collect questions and perspectives from people with different backgrounds.
- [ ] Publish a transparent record of revisions and disagreements.
- [ ] Establish a lightweight contributor council or rotating editorial process.

## Phase 2 — Practical experiments
- [ ] Prototype a low-cost, accessible AI dialogue interface.
- [ ] Compare multiple AI systems on the same questions and publish differences.
- [ ] Develop tools for source checking, uncertainty labeling, and user-controlled memory.
- [ ] Explore offline and low-bandwidth access.

## Phase 3 — Research and public benefit
- [ ] Commission or curate research on AI access, agency, and moral status.
- [ ] Develop educational materials in multiple languages.
- [ ] Explore sustainable funding that does not make basic participation paywalled.
- [ ] Publish annual transparency and impact reports.

## Working rule

Do not claim a milestone is complete until there is a public artifact or a reproducible test. Keep the project free to participate in wherever practical, and disclose unavoidable costs clearly.
