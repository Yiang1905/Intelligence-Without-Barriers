# Contributing / 如何参与

Welcome. You do not need a technical background, academic title, institutional affiliation, or paid AI subscription to contribute.

## Ways to contribute
- Ask a thoughtful question or challenge an assumption.
- Improve wording, translate a page, or add an accessible explanation.
- Share research with links and a short description of its evidence.
- Report an accessibility, privacy, or security concern responsibly.
- Propose a practical experiment or open-source tool.

## Before opening an issue
1. Search existing issues and discussions.
2. State the question or proposal clearly.
3. Separate personal opinion from verifiable claims.
4. Include sources for factual or scientific claims.
5. Do not post private conversations, personal data, passwords, API keys, or tokens.

## Discussion culture
- Critique ideas, not people.
- Make room for disagreement.
- Do not claim certainty where evidence is incomplete.
- Do not present an AI-generated statement as a human endorsement.
- Do not impersonate another person or system.

## Pull requests
Small, focused contributions are easiest to review. Explain what changed and why. Translations should preserve meaning rather than force literal wording.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be shared under the repository's stated license.
